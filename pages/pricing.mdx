# Pricing

Start free, no card. Upgrade in the [dashboard](/dashboard/billing) when you need
more, and cancel there at any time.

| | Free | Starter | Pro |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Price | $0 | **$9 / month** | **$29 / month** |
| Units per month | 1,000 | 25,000 | 250,000 |
| Rate limit | 2 requests/s | 5 requests/s | 20 requests/s |
| Email, domain, website, SEO, QR, and developer utility APIs | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| AI endpoints (content, regex, stacktrace, README, changelog) | No | No | Yes |
| Test key allowance per month | 100 | 200 | 500 |

Prices are in US dollars. Payments are handled by
[Lemon Squeezy](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com), which acts as the merchant of record
and adds sales tax or VAT where it applies.

## What a unit is

Most calls cost **1 unit**. Calls that do more work cost more:

| Endpoint | Units |
|---|---:|
| Single checks: email validation, DNS, SSL, security headers, robots.txt, sitemap, CORS, URL safety, QR codes, and the developer utilities | 1 per call |
| [Bulk email verification](/api/bulk-email-verification) (JSON or CSV), disposable and role-account checks | 1 per address |
| Domain health | 1 per domain |
| [SEO site audit](/api/seo-site-audit) | 10 per call |
| AI endpoints (Pro) | 20 per call |

A bulk call with 300 addresses costs 300 units, the same as 300 single calls.

**Failed requests are almost always free.** Units are refunded for validation
errors (`422`), requests refused for your plan (`403`), and any server error
(`5xx`). Requests rejected for a bad key, the rate limit, or the quota are never
charged in the first place. The exception is `400 BAD_REQUEST`, such as an
unreadable CSV upload, which uses the call's units. See [Errors](/errors).

## When the month resets

- **Free:** on the 1st of each month, at 00:00 UTC.
- **Starter and Pro:** on your renewal date.

The dashboard's [Usage](/dashboard/usage) page shows how much of the month you've
used. Every successful response also carries an `X-Quota-Remaining` header. When
the quota runs out, requests return `429 QUOTA_EXCEEDED` until the month resets or
you upgrade. There are no overage charges: you are never billed for more than your
plan.

## Test keys

Test keys (`apim_test_...`) have their own allowance from the table above and
never use your live units. See [Authentication](/authentication#live-and-test-keys).

## Changing or cancelling

- **Upgrade:** choose a plan on the [Billing](/dashboard/billing) page. The new
  limits apply within a minute of payment.
- **Cancel or change card:** use **Manage subscription** on the Billing page,
  which opens the Lemon Squeezy customer portal. A cancelled plan keeps working
  until the end of the period you paid for, then the account returns to Free.
- **Failed payment:** the plan keeps working for 7 days while the payment is
  retried.

Questions about billing: [support@apimask.dev](mailto:support@apimask.dev).
