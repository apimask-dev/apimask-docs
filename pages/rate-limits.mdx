# Rate limits and quotas

ApiMask applies two kinds of limits to your account:

- A **rate limit**, which caps how fast you can call.
- A **monthly quota** of units, which caps how much you can use. See
  [Pricing](/pricing#what-a-unit-is) for what a unit is.

Both apply to the whole account, across every key, not to each key separately.

## Limits per plan

| | Free | Starter | Pro |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Requests per second | 2 | 5 | 20 |
| Short burst | 4 | 10 | 40 |
| Requests per minute | 60 | 300 | 900 |
| Requests in flight at once | 2 | 5 | 20 |
| Units per month | 1,000 | 25,000 | 250,000 |
| Test key: requests per second | 1 | 1 | 2 |
| Test key: units per month | 100 | 200 | 500 |

The burst lets a short spike through, for example a page that fires a few
requests at once, as long as the average stays under the per-second rate.

## Response headers

Requests that pass the limits carry two headers:

| Header | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `RateLimit-Limit` | Requests per second your plan allows. |
| `X-Quota-Remaining` | Units left this month for the kind of key you used (live or test). |

## When you hit a limit

| Response | Cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| `429 RATE_LIMITED` with `Retry-After: 1` | Over the per-second or per-minute rate | Wait a second and retry, with backoff |
| `429 CONCURRENCY_LIMITED` | Too many requests running at once | Lower your client's parallelism |
| `429 QUOTA_EXCEEDED` | Monthly units used up | Wait for the reset, or [upgrade](/dashboard/billing) |

None of these use any units. Free quotas reset on the 1st of each month at
00:00 UTC; paid quotas reset on your renewal date.

## Sending many addresses

To check a list, use the [bulk endpoints](/api/bulk-email-verification). They
take up to 500 addresses per call (1 unit each), so a 10,000-row list is 20
calls instead of 10,000, and the rate limit stops being the bottleneck.
